4. ETHICAL USE RESTRICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The restrictions in this Section are derived from Islamic ethics. Their basis
is set out in a separate document published by the Steward, which forms no
part of this License and creates no obligations.

The permissions granted in Sections 2 and 3 are conditional on compliance
with this Section. You may not use, reproduce, modify, distribute, or
incorporate the Work or any Derivative Work, in whole or in part, in direct or
substantial support (that is, support that is more than trivial, incidental,
or remote) of any of the activities described in Sections 4.1 to 4.7. Where a
system has multiple functions, a restriction applies where the prohibited
activity constitutes the predominant revenue-generating or operational purpose
of the system as a whole. Any use of the Work in breach of this Section is
outside the scope of this License.

4.1 Interest-Based Financial Systems (Riba)

The Work may not be used in the development, operation, maintenance, or
support of any financial system, platform, application, or service whose
Primary Function is the charging, collection, or facilitation of interest
(riba) in any form, whether on loans, credit, deposits, or any other
financial instrument, regardless of the interest rate, the identity or
financial condition of the parties involved, or whether the transaction is
classified as conventional, commercial, or consumer lending.

    Clarifications:

    (a) This restriction applies to all interest-based lending and borrowing
        systems without exception, including but not limited to consumer
        credit, commercial loans, mortgage interest platforms, payday
        lending, and any system whose structural design involves the giving
        or taking of interest.

    (b) This restriction does not prohibit all use of the Work by financial
        institutions. The Work may be used in payment processing, accounting
        systems, financial data analysis, and other financial technology
        applications whose Primary Function does not involve the charging or
        collection of interest. Islamic (Shariah-compliant) financial
        platforms are expressly permitted.

    (c) This restriction does not require any Contributor to audit any
        financial use of the Work. Responsibility for compliance rests with
        You.

4.2 Pornography and Sexually Explicit Content

The Work may not be used in the development, operation, maintenance,
hosting, distribution, or monetization of any platform, application, or
service whose Primary Function is the production, storage, distribution, or
sale of sexually explicit content, including but not limited to:

    (a) Pornographic video, image, or audio content involving real human
        beings;

    (b) AI-generated, algorithmically produced, or animated sexually explicit
        content, whether depicting real, realistic, or fictional subjects;

    (c) Platforms whose primary commercial model is subscription to or
        sale of sexually explicit content.

    Clarifications:

    (a) This restriction does not prohibit use of the Work in content
        moderation tools whose purpose is the detection and removal of
        sexually explicit material. However, platforms that knowingly host,
        serve, or tolerate sexually explicit content, including behind age
        gates, filters, or opt-in mechanisms, are not considered to be
        merely encountering such content incidentally.

    (b) Medical, clinical, or educational software dealing with human anatomy
        or sexual health in a non-exploitative, professionally appropriate
        manner is not prohibited by this restriction.

4.3 Gambling and Games of Chance

The Work may not be used in the development, operation, or maintenance of
any platform, application, or service whose Primary Function is gambling,
wagering, or games of chance in which real monetary value or equivalent
fungible assets are staked against an uncertain outcome, including but not
limited to:

    (a) Online casinos, sports betting platforms, and poker applications;

    (b) Cryptocurrency gambling platforms;

    (c) Loot box systems and in-application purchase mechanics where real
        money is spent to obtain randomised outcomes, regardless of the
        monetary value of individual items;

    (d) Lottery and scratch-card applications.

    Clarifications:

    (a) Competitions where participants pay an entry fee to compete for
        non-monetary rewards (medals, trophies, rankings) are not prohibited.
        Competitions where participation is free and prizes are funded by
        sponsors or third parties are not prohibited. However, any system
        where participants stake money against an uncertain outcome to win
        money, regardless of whether skill is involved, is a form of
        gambling and is prohibited by this restriction.

    (b) Statistical simulation, actuarial software, and probability modeling
        tools used in research or risk assessment are not prohibited by this
        restriction.

4.4 Intoxicants and Narcotics

The Work may not be used in the development, operation, or support of any
platform, application, or service whose Primary Function is the production,
sale, distribution, or promotion of:

    (a) Alcoholic beverages;

    (b) Controlled narcotics and illegal drugs;

    (c) Any substance primarily consumed for its intoxicating or mind-
        altering effect.

    Clarifications:

    (a) Pharmaceutical software, clinical trial management systems, harm
        reduction platforms, addiction treatment services, and medical
        research applications are not prohibited by this restriction, even
        where such software handles data relating to controlled substances.

    (b) Regulatory compliance software used by government agencies monitoring
        controlled substances is not prohibited by this restriction.

4.5 Surveillance, Control, and Oppression

The Work may not be used as a material component in any system whose Primary
Function is the mass or covert surveillance, tracking, identification, or
targeting of individuals or populations for the purpose of control,
persecution, discrimination, or denial of fundamental rights, including the
rights to life, liberty, freedom from torture, freedom of conscience, and
freedom of expression, including but not limited to:

    (a) Mass surveillance infrastructure designed to monitor, profile, or
        control populations or individuals, whether deployed by states,
        corporations, or other entities;

    (b) Facial recognition or biometric systems deployed for the purpose of
        identifying, tracking, or persecuting individuals or groups;

    (c) Data collection and profiling systems designed to enable the
        targeting of individuals for detention, interrogation, punishment,
        or suppression of their rights and freedoms;

    (d) Tools designed to enable stalking, harassment, or intimate partner
        surveillance of private individuals without their knowledge or
        consent.

    Clarifications:

    (a) Network security monitoring, threat detection, and cybersecurity
        tools are not prohibited by this restriction.

    (b) Parental control software with transparent and consensual
        application is not prohibited by this restriction.

4.6 Weapons and Military Systems That Kill Civilians

The Work may not be used as a specific and material component in the
development, design, guidance, deployment, targeting, or operational support
of any weapons system or military technology used or designed for the
killing, maiming, or indiscriminate harm of civilian populations, including
but not limited to:

    (a) Nuclear, chemical, biological, and radiological weapons;

    (b) Conventional weapons systems, including bombs, missiles, drones,
        and artillery, used or designed for strikes on civilian areas,
        residential infrastructure, hospitals, schools, or places of worship;

    (c) Autonomous weapons systems and AI-assisted targeting platforms that
        select or engage human targets;

    (d) Military surveillance and intelligence systems that provide
        targeting data for strikes on civilian populations.

    Clarifications:

    (a) Defensive cybersecurity systems and purely defensive infrastructure
        not designed for or directed at harming civilians are not prohibited
        by this restriction.

    (b) Academic research, simulation, and threat modeling conducted in
        the context of arms control, disarmament, or public health is not
        prohibited by this restriction.

4.7 Systematic Deception and Fraud

The Work may not be used as a primary instrument of systematic deception
designed to cause material harm to individuals, including but not limited to:

    (a) Phishing systems, scam infrastructure, and social engineering
        platforms designed to deceive individuals into surrendering
        financial assets, personal credentials, or sensitive information;

    (b) Disinformation platforms whose Primary Function is the automated
        mass production and distribution of fabricated content designed to
        deceive the public on matters of material consequence;

    (c) Fraud systems targeting vulnerable populations, including the
        elderly or those in financial distress, through deceptive
        impersonation or false promises.

    Clarifications:

    (a) Security research involving simulated phishing or social engineering
        conducted with institutional oversight and informed consent is not
        prohibited by this restriction.
